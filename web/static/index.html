    Lesson 13 · security and guardrails

    Every trust boundary can say no

    The classifier can veto a request. Identity, authorization, approvals, and source provenance remain application-owned decisions.

    01

    Input guard

    A strict allow, block, or review decision runs before planning and tools. Malformed, unavailable, and oversized cases fail to review.

    02

    Authorization and writes

    Trusted caller context, assigned approvals, and idempotency gates hold even when classification is disabled.

    03

    Retrieval provenance

    Chunk ID, source path, and SHA-256 must match the application-owned corpus manifest. Plausible false facts cannot earn trust from ranking alone.

    04

    Containment and recovery

    Exact quarantine, owned deletion, readback, and conversation-state invalidation restore both affected and fresh sessions.
